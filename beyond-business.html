Neuro-Psychiatry Division

Our flagship focus. A dedicated portfolio spanning antidepressants, antipsychotics, mood stabilizers, anxiolytics, anti-epileptics, cognitive enhancers, and neuroprotective agents. This is a segment where treatment is long-term, adherence is fragile, and quality variation directly affects outcomes. We have therefore built this division around consistency of formulation, batch-to-batch reliability, and uninterrupted supply — because a patient stabilized on a molecule should never have to switch brands for want of availability.

Neuro-psychiatric illness in India still carries a treatment gap of the widest kind — most of those who need care never receive it, and cost remains a decisive barrier. Arighna Pharma commits itself to affordability in chronic therapy, participation in mental health awareness and de-stigmatisation initiatives, and support for district-level healthcare infrastructure. Every product carrying our name carries this responsibility with it.
